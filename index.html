Frequently Asked Questions

A few useful things to know before your ORRIA piece arrives.

What is Jesmonite?

Jesmonite is a versatile mineral-based composite known for its smooth finish and ability to create beautiful contemporary forms.

Are ORRIA pieces handmade?

Yes. ORRIA pieces are designed for small-batch making and carefully finished to give every piece its own character.

How should I care for my piece?

Wipe gently with a soft, dry or slightly damp cloth. Avoid soaking, harsh chemicals and abrasive cleaners.

How can I track my order?

Use the Track Order option with your ORRIA order number and the mobile number used at checkout.

Can I request a custom piece?

Yes. Use the Custom Orders section and send your requirements to ORRIA on WhatsApp.

OUR STORY

Made slowly. Made thoughtfully.

ORRIA creates small-batch Jesmonite décor with a focus on warm neutrals, tactile finishes and thoughtful personalisation. Each piece is designed to feel at home in your space and meaningful when gifted.

ORRIA INFORMATION

Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Orders are carefully packed before dispatch. Delivery timelines and charges will be confirmed with you on WhatsApp at checkout until the final shipping partner is connected.

Returns

Because ORRIA pieces are handmade and some are personalised, return eligibility depends on the product and its condition. Please contact us with photos if an item arrives damaged.

Care

Keep Jesmonite pieces dry when possible, wipe gently with a soft cloth, and avoid harsh chemicals or prolonged soaking.
