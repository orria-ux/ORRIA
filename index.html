OUR STORY

Made slowly. Made thoughtfully.

ORRIA creates small-batch Jesmonite décor with a focus on warm neutrals, tactile finishes and thoughtful personalisation. Each piece is designed to feel at home in your space and meaningful when gifted.

ORRIA INFORMATION

Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Orders are carefully packed before dispatch. Delivery timelines and charges will be confirmed with you on WhatsApp at checkout until the final shipping partner is connected.

Returns

Because ORRIA pieces are handmade and some are personalised, return eligibility depends on the product and its condition. Please contact us with photos if an item arrives damaged.

Care

Keep Jesmonite pieces dry when possible, wipe gently with a soft cloth, and avoid harsh chemicals or prolonged soaking.
